Quick Setup Guide

🚀 Getting Started with VEYRA

Adding VEYRA to your server takes about 90 seconds. Follow these 3 practical steps to make sure all permissions and protections work smoothly.

Step 1: Invite the Bot with Administrator Rights

Click the official OAuth2 authorization link below to invite VEYRA into your Discord guild:

Invite VEYRA to Your Server ↗

Why Administrator? VEYRA monitors audit logs, restores deleted channels, and manages tickets across multiple categories. Administrator permission ensures it can intervene immediately when a rogue account attempts to damage the server.

Step 2: Position the VEYRA Role in Server Settings

Discord's Role Hierarchy Rule: A bot cannot manage, punish, or revoke roles from any user whose highest role is equal to or above the bot's own role.

1. Open your Discord server, click the server name, and go to Server Settings → Roles.
2. Find the VEYRA role in the list.
3. Click and drag the VEYRA role up near the top — just beneath your personal Server Owner role and strictly above all Moderator and Admin roles.
4. Click Save Changes.

Step 3: Run /scan & Arm Protections

Go to any staff channel where VEYRA can read messages and type:

/scan

VEYRA will immediately audit your roles, permissions, and channel setups, letting you know if any untrusted roles have dangerous permissions (like Mention Everyone or Manage Webhooks).

Step 4: Connect the Web Dashboard

Visit the VEYRA Dashboard, log in with your Discord account, and pick your server. You can customize bot nicknames, welcome messages, ticket panels, and giveaway embeds with live real-time previews.

Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the most common setup and troubleshooting questions.

1. The bot is in my server but doesn't answer commands. Why?
This is almost always a channel permission issue. Ensure that VEYRA has View Channel, Send Messages, and Embed Links in that channel. Also make sure Slash Commands are enabled in Discord under Server Settings → Integrations → VEYRA.
2. How do I add my head moderators to the Anti-Nuke whitelist?
As the server owner, run /whitelist add_user user:@YourStaff. Whitelisted members can delete channels or manage roles without triggering the bot's auto-ban defense. You can review all whitelisted accounts anytime by typing /whitelist show.
3. How does the Kitt Voice greeting work?
When any member joins a voice channel, VEYRA joins automatically, announces that the user has entered with clear audio (e.g. "Alex has joined the voice channel"), and then disconnects after 4 seconds of silence so it doesn't linger in your call.
4. Do settings I change on the website save permanently?
Yes. All settings — custom nicknames, command prefixes, welcome cards, ticket panels, and sticky messages — are saved directly into the database. When you reload or switch servers, your updated settings stay intact.
5. Where are ticket transcripts sent when a ticket closes?
When a ticket is closed with /ticket close, VEYRA compiles the full conversation into a clean HTML file. The file is sent directly to the member's private DMs and to your designated ticket logs channel on the server.
6. How do I change the bot's command prefix?
Open your web dashboard, navigate to Bot Settings, type your preferred prefix character (e.g. !, ?, .), and click Add Prefix. You can have multiple prefixes active simultaneously.
Protection Engine

🛡️ Security & Anti-Nuke Suite

How VEYRA protects your server from rogue administrators, compromised accounts, webhook spam, and raid bots.

How Anti-Nuke Works in Plain English

Discord raids and nukes usually happen when a staff account gets token-logged or an angry moderator tries to wipe a server. Standard bots poll audit logs on a delay — by then, dozens of channels are already gone.

VEYRA listens to Discord events directly. The instant an unauthorized user deletes a channel, edits a role, or creates a spam webhook, VEYRA acts in under 0.04 milliseconds:

  • Bans or strips the attacker: The rogue account is instantly banned and stripped of administrative roles.
  • Restores the deleted asset: Deleted channels and roles are recreated with their exact permissions restored.
  • Alerts your staff: An emergency incident report is dispatched to your security logs channel.

Active Protection Modules

Protection Module What Triggers It What VEYRA Does
Anti-Channel Delete An untrusted user deletes any server channel Instantly recreates the channel, restores its permissions, and bans the attacker.
Anti-Role Delete An untrusted user deletes or strips roles Recreates the role and revokes the attacker's administrative permissions.
Anti-Webhook Hijacking A rogue user or bot creates a webhook Immediately deletes the webhook before spam can occur and bans the creator.
Anti-Bot Add An admin invites an unverified or rogue bot Instantly kicks or bans the unapproved bot and punishes the admin who added it.
Anti-Mass Ban / Kick More than 3 members banned/kicked in 10s Freezes moderation actions and permanently bans the compromised account.

Support System

🎫 Support & Buy Ticket System

Set up clean button panels, intake modals, private staff channels, and automatic HTML transcripts.

Setting Up Your First Ticket Panel

1. Create a channel where members will open tickets (e.g. #create-a-ticket).
2. Type the command below in that channel:

/setup panel channel:#create-a-ticket

3. VEYRA will spawn an interactive card with a button (e.g. "📩 Open Ticket").
4. When a member clicks the button, a private channel is created (e.g. #ticket-0012) visible only to that user and your designated staff roles.
5. Once the issue is resolved, staff can run /ticket close to automatically save an HTML transcript and clean up the channel.

Ticket Management Commands

Command Example Description

/ticket add /ticket add user:@Partner Adds an additional member or role to view and speak in the ticket.
/ticket remove /ticket remove user:@Troll Removes a member from the active ticket channel.
/ticket transcript /ticket transcript Exports a complete HTML transcript of the ticket without closing it.

Sticky Messages (Keep Rules Visible)

Sticky messages automatically re-post at the very bottom of a channel every time people talk. This ensures rules, verification links, or announcements are always the last thing members see.

You can create sticky messages directly on your Dashboard under Sticky Messages by selecting a channel and typing your reminder message.
