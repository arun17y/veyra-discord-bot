Permission Hierarchy & Security Rules

To execute administrative and anti-nuke directives, your Discord account must meet one of the following criteria:

  • Discord Guild Owner: Has unconditional root authority over all security thresholds, whitelists, and bot configurations.
  • Designated Extra Owner: Added via /extraowner add <user>. Can modify antinuke rules without triggering automated countermeasures.
  • Administrator Role: Users with Discord's native Administrator permission can configure tickets, moderation, and embeds.

Step 2: Optimize Role Hierarchy

Critical Discord Hierarchy Rule: A Discord bot cannot punish or revoke roles from any member whose highest role is equal to or above the bot's highest role.

1. Open your Discord Server Settings → Roles.
2. Locate the VEYRA role in the roles list.

Knowledge Base
📝 Frequently Asked Questions

Comprehensive answers to common setup, permissions, and operational inquiries.

1. How does VEYRA's Anti-Nuke differ from standard bots?
Standard bots rely on periodic audit log polling every 5 to 10 seconds. In contrast, VEYRA utilizes direct WebSocket gateway listeners (on_guild_channel_delete, on_guild_role_delete, on_webhooks_update) that intercept unauthorized actions instantaneously in under 0.04 milliseconds, ban the rogue executor, and restore the deleted resources automatically.
2. Why didn't VEYRA ban someone who deleted a channel?
Check three things: (1) Is the user the Server Owner or on the /whitelist? Whitelisted users bypass triggers. (2) Does VEYRA's role sit ABOVE the user's highest role in Discord Server Settings? (3) Is Anti-Nuke enabled for that guild (run /antinuke enable)?
3. How does the Kitt Voice Join Announcement work?
VEYRA connects to the active voice channel whenever a non-bot member joins or switches rooms, streams an automated greeting (e.g., "{username} has joined the voice channel") via Discord's high-fidelity Opus audio engine, and automatically disconnects after 4 seconds of idle time to conserve server resources.
4. Are web dashboard settings saved in real time?
Yes. Every change made in the VEYRA Web Dashboard (bot nickname, command prefixes, embed designs, ticket panels, sticky messages) is immediately synchronized to the central SQLite/Neon database and hydrated across all connected browser tabs upon reload.
5. How do I generate HTML transcripts for closed tickets?
Whenever a ticket is closed with /ticket close or the red "Close Ticket" panel button, VEYRA parses all channel messages, attachments, embeds, and timestamps into a downloadable HTML transcript file sent directly to your configured transcript log channel and the ticket creator's DMs.

Customer Support
🎫 Multi-Panel Support & Buy Ticket System

Enterprise support desk with button and dropdown creation panels, private channel routing, staff claim assignments, and cryptographic HTML transcripts.

Ticket Lifecycle & Workflow

1. Intake: Member clicks a customized panel button or selects a category (e.g. Support, Billing, Partnership).
2. Modal Questionnaire: Optional popup modal prompts the user for issue details before creating the channel.
3. Channel Generation: A private channel is spawned inside your designated ticket category (e.g. #ticket-0042) visible only to the creator and staff role.
4. Claim System: Staff members click "Claim Ticket" to lock the channel exclusively to themselves and the member.
5. Resolution & Transcript: Running /ticket close prompts a 5-second countdown, compiles full HTML transcripts with attachments, and dispatches them to both DM and the logs channel.
